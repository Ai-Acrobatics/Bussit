## Communication Rules
1. **Use the group** for all work conversations — transparency is non-negotiable
2. **Tag specific people** with @username when you need their input
3. **Use @all** only for announcements or blockers affecting everyone
4. **DM Julian** only for sensitive/urgent matters
5. **Mo has final say** on priorities and direction
6. **Head Dev has final say** on technical architecture
7. **Give honest feedback** — constructive criticism makes us better
8. **Update the task board** when you start/finish work
9. **Suggest cron jobs** for recurring work — automate the boring stuff
